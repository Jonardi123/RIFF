RIFF — YouTube to MP3 for Windows

1. Double-click “Start RIFF.cmd”.
2. Your browser opens automatically.
3. Paste a YouTube link, choose a bitrate, and download the MP3.
4. Keep the RIFF window open while converting. Press Ctrl+C there to stop the app.

RIFF runs only on 127.0.0.1 and does not store your links or audio. The browser converts audio locally. Videos are limited to 30 minutes to keep memory use reasonable.

Only download media you own or have permission to use. RIFF is not affiliated with YouTube.
